# Hi, I'm George Boateng-Sarfo, PhD

### Computational Biologist | Bioinformatician | Bioinformatics Software Developer

I'm a computational biologist and bioinformatics software developer
with research interests in RNA biology, gene regulation, genomics,
epitranscriptomics, and computational method development.

My work focuses on developing computational tools and reproducible
bioinformatics pipelines to investigate RNA processing, alternative
splicing, alternative polyadenylation, RNA modifications, and their
roles in biological processes and human diseases.

I am particularly interested in integrating bulk RNA-seq,
single-cell transcriptomics, long-read sequencing, and computational
models to understand complex mechanisms of post-transcriptional
gene regulation.
